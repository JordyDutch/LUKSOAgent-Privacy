LUKSO Agent Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

LUKSO Agent provides AI-assisted community answers and report-based moderation on Discord and Telegram. This policy explains how the bot processes information. The operator and privacy contact is JordyDutch, reachable on Discord as @JordyDutch. The bot is not operated by Discord or Telegram.

Information processed

The bot may process messages addressed to it, reported messages, and relevant conversation context that the platform permits it to access. This can include message text, links, supported images and attachments, contact cards, polls, checklists and button labels or destinations. Supported content differs by platform; unavailable evidence is not treated as proof of a violation.

Associated information can include user IDs and display names, server or group IDs, channel and message IDs, timestamps, reply relationships, and permissions or roles needed to check whether an action is allowed. The bot also keeps operational records, conversation context and moderation records, such as report references, decision reasons, action status and duplicate-action prevention records.

Please do not send passwords, access tokens, recovery phrases, payment details or other sensitive information to the bot. A report may cause the reported message to be reviewed even if its author did not directly address the bot.

Why information is used

Information is used to answer community questions, understand the message being reported, apply the community's rules, prevent repeated or unsafe actions, investigate failures, and respond to support or privacy requests.

Moderation is initiated by a report or moderation request, not a continuous AI scan of every message. Ordinary mentions can still receive conversational answers. A member's accusation alone is not proof of spam or abuse.

Where the evidence and permissions support an action, the bot may delete the specific offending message and restrict its author. The configured Telegram action is a permanent mute; the configured Discord action can be a ban. The bot does not purge the member's message history. Confirmed actions and their reasons can be announced publicly in the affected chat. Ask a human community administrator to review a decision you believe is wrong.

AI processing and sharing

Relevant content and context can be sent to external AI services to generate an answer or assess a report. Configured model routes use OpenAI and Anthropic; an answer may also involve an external search service when research is necessary. Information is processed on the operator's server and by the services used for that request, which may be outside your country.

The operator does not use Discord or Telegram messages to train or fine-tune AI models. Model-training use is disabled for the AI accounts used by the bot. Generating an answer or moderation assessment is inference, not model training.

The operator does not sell message data or provide it to advertising networks or data brokers. Access is limited to operating and supporting the bot, the service providers needed for those functions, and disclosures required by law. Discord, Telegram and AI providers also have their own policies for their services.

Storage and retention

Some information is stored outside Discord and Telegram on the operator's server. This includes saved conversation context, operational records, moderation records and recovery backups. Administrative access is restricted.

Eligible inactive conversation sessions are configured for cleanup after seven days. This is not a seven-day limit for every message: ongoing conversations, retained context or memory, diagnostic records, moderation records and backups may remain longer. There is currently no universal guarantee that every copy is removed within 30 days.

Information should be retained only while needed for the purposes above or where retention is legally required. Privacy requests include reviewing relevant active data, retained context and backups. Removing a visible message from Discord or Telegram does not by itself guarantee immediate removal of all copies already processed by the bot.

Access, deletion and objections

Contact @JordyDutch on Discord to request access, correction or deletion of your information, to object to processing, or to raise a privacy concern. If you cannot send a direct message, ask an administrator in a community where the bot is present to connect you with the operator. Do not post private information in a public chat or GitHub issue.

Identify the platform, your account, and the relevant community or message links if available. The operator may ask for minimal information to verify that the request concerns your account. Never send account passwords, tokens or identity documents to the bot.

Requests are handled manually. There is no verified self-service command that immediately stops all processing or erases every stored copy. Avoiding direct conversations with the bot does not prevent another member from reporting a message you posted. Community administrators can remove the bot or restrict its access to channels.

The operator will address requests promptly and explain any restriction or legally required retention. Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to restrict processing, receive a copy of your data, or complain to your local data protection authority.

Changes

This policy will be updated when the bot's data handling changes. The date above identifies the current version. Contact the operator if you need clarification before using the bot.
